# AI Content and Search Optimization

Google's guidance on AI-generated content and optimizing for AI features in Search.

## What Google says about AI-generated content

Google does not penalize content simply because it was created with AI tools. The key requirements:

1. **Meet Search Essentials**: AI content must follow the same rules as any other content
2. **Don't do scaled content abuse**: Using AI to generate many pages without adding user value violates spam policies
3. **Focus on accuracy, quality, and relevance**: Especially for metadata, structured data, and alt text
4. **Provide context**: If AI is used, consider sharing how it was used

### What matters

- The content provides genuine value to users
- It demonstrates expertise or unique perspective
- It is accurate and well-maintained
- It is not mass-produced without quality control

### What doesn't matter

- Whether content was written by a human or assisted by AI
- The specific tools used to create content

## What Google says about AI features in Search

Google Search now includes AI Overviews and AI Mode. These features:

- Use **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** to ground responses in web content
- Use **query fan-out** to gather information from multiple sources
- Still rely on the same core ranking systems

### How to be visible in AI features

The same SEO best practices apply:

- **Create valuable, non-commodity content**: Unique perspectives and genuine expertise stand out
- **Build clear technical structure**: Content must be crawlable and indexable
- **Provide good page experience**: Fast, secure, mobile-friendly pages
- **Use structured data**: Helps Google understand content (but isn't required for AI features)

### What to ignore

- **LLMS.txt files**: Google Search doesn't use them
- **"Chunking" content**: Google can understand topics across a page
- **Rewriting content for AI systems**: Google understands synonyms and meaning
- **Seeking inauthentic "mentions"**: Focus on genuine quality instead

## What this means for this website

This site's content is personal writing — essays, notes, and reading reflections. This is inherently non-commodity content. To maintain visibility:

- Continue writing from genuine experience and perspective
- Don't create content只是为了 targeting search queries
- If AI tools assist in drafting or editing, ensure the final content reflects genuine expertise
- The site's structured data (Article, Breadcrumb) helps Google understand content type

## Checklist

- [ ] Content provides genuine value and unique perspective
- [ ] If AI tools are used, the content still demonstrates expertise
- [ ] No mass-produced or thin AI content
- [ ] Structured data is accurate and complete
- [ ] Content is crawlable and indexable
