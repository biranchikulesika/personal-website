# Creating Helpful, Reliable, People-First Content

Google's ranking systems prioritize content that is helpful, reliable, and created for people — not content created to manipulate search rankings.

## What Google says

Google evaluates content against these criteria:

### Content quality
- Does it provide original information, analysis, or insights?
- Is it comprehensive and substantial?
- Is it the sort of page you'd want to bookmark or share?
- Does it provide value compared to other pages in search results?

### Expertise
- Does it present information in a way that builds trust (clear sourcing, author information)?
- Is it written by someone who demonstrably knows the topic?
- Does it have factual errors?

### People-first vs. search engine-first

**People-first** (good):
- You have an intended audience that would find it useful
- It demonstrates first-hand expertise
- It leaves readers feeling they've learned something

**Search engine-first** (bad):
- Created primarily to attract search engine visits
- Mass-produced content on many topics
- Summarizing others without adding value
- Writing about trending topics just for traffic

## What this means for this website

This is a personal website. The content is inherently people-first because it reflects personal writing, thinking, and expertise. The key is to maintain this:

- **Essays** should provide genuine analysis and personal insight, not just restate common knowledge
- **Notes** should be authentic thinking artifacts, not SEO-optimized content
- **Library entries** should reflect actual reading and genuine recommendations
- **Now page** should reflect real current focus

## E-E-A-T (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness)

E-E-A-T is **not** a direct ranking factor. However, Google's systems use signals that align with E-E-A-T to assess content quality. For this site:

- **Experience**: First-hand perspective from actually building, reading, and thinking
- **Expertise**: Demonstrated through the author's work and writing
- **Authoritativeness**: Established through consistent, quality content
- **Trustworthiness**: Built through honest, transparent writing

The site's `WebSite` structured data includes `knowsAbout` fields that declare the author's areas of expertise.

## AI-generated content

Google does not penalize AI-generated content per se. The key requirements:

- Content must meet Search Essentials standards
- Focus on accuracy, quality, and relevance
- Don't use AI to generate many pages without adding value (scaled content abuse)
- If AI tools are used, the content should still demonstrate expertise and provide unique value

See [AI Content Guidance](ai-content-guidance.md).

## Checklist

- [ ] Content is written primarily for human readers
- [ ] Content demonstrates first-hand experience or genuine expertise
- [ ] Content provides value beyond what's already available
- [ ] Titles accurately describe the content
- [ ] Author information is clear and associated with the content
