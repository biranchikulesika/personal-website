# Video SEO

If your site includes video content, following video SEO best practices helps Google find and understand it.

## What Google says

### Best practices

1. **Embed video on a standalone page** — with a descriptive title and text context
2. **Use descriptive titles and descriptions** — keywords should be natural
3. **Provide a thumbnail image** — attractive and representative
4. **Use VideoObject structured data** — helps Google understand the video
5. **Make video directly accessible** — avoid requiring login or special players

### VideoObject structured data

Required properties:
- `name`: Video title
- `description`: Video description
- `thumbnailUrl`: Thumbnail image URL
- `uploadDate`: ISO 8601 date

## What this means for this website

This site does not currently host video content. If video content is added in the future:

- Create a dedicated page for each video
- Add `VideoObject` structured data
- Include descriptive titles and descriptions
