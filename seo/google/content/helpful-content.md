# Helpful Content

Google's systems prioritize content that is helpful, reliable, and created for people.

## What Google says

### People-first content checklist

Answer yes to these questions:
- Do you have an audience that would find this content useful?
- Does it demonstrate first-hand expertise?
- Does your site have a primary focus?
- Will readers feel they've learned something?
- Will readers have a satisfying experience?

### Search engine-first content warnings

Red flags:
- Content primarily made to attract search engine visits
- Mass-produced content on many topics
- Summarizing others without adding value
- Writing about trending topics just for traffic
- Writing to a specific word count because "Google prefers it" (they don't)

### E-E-A-T signals

While E-E-A-T isn't a direct ranking factor, these signals matter:
- **Experience**: First-hand use of a product, service, or topic
- **Expertise**: Demonstrated knowledge in the subject
- **Authoritativeness**: Recognition as a go-to source
- **Trustworthiness**: Accuracy, transparency, honesty

## What this means for this website

This is a personal website where content is inherently people-first:
- **Essays** reflect genuine thinking and analysis
- **Notes** are authentic thinking artifacts
- **Library entries** are based on actual reading
- **Now page** reflects real current focus

The key is to maintain authenticity and avoid creating content purely for search visibility.

## Checklist

- [ ] Content is written for human readers first
- [ ] Content demonstrates genuine expertise or experience
- [ ] Content provides unique value or perspective
- [ ] Content is well-organized and easy to read
- [ ] No mass-produced or thin content
